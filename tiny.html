PEQA LabSimulatorTiny instance

Parameters

Brute-force check: 3 graders, 3 papers, discrete reports

Graders A, B, C own papers 1, 2, 3 and never grade their own. Papers 1–2 are probes (true scores (2.5, 3.0) and (2.0, 3.0)); paper 3 is the only non-probe (true (2.5, 2.0)), graded by A and B and regraded by C. Each paper has 2 questions. A signal = truth + noise from {−1, −½, 0, ½, 1} (probabilities from a discretised normal; effort sets its sd). Grader A picks an effort (low/high) and a shift added to her reports (0 = truthful). All 5⁸ = 390,625 signal profiles are enumerated exactly; B is honest with medium effort. Median uses a phantom report at the prior mean μ.
