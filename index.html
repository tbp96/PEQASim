PEQA LabSimulatorTiny instance Peer grading with bias, noise, adversaries and incentives (Chakraborty–Jindal–Nath, PEQA)

Parameters

One random round with your parameters. Assignment matrix: rows = graders, columns = papers; red P = probe (graded by staff), blue N = non-probe; a grader never gets her own paper. Estimates: PEQA measures each grader's bias b̂ and reliability τ̂ from her probes only. Scores: the final score of every paper under each mechanism (probe papers get the staff score under PEQA).

Pick an experiment. Each point is the average of Replications random rounds; RMSE is measured on non-probe papers.

Uses the effort-game parameters (left) with all other graders honest and equally precise. The focal grader changes her own bias and reliability.
